FAQ

Questions parents ask

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp. We're happy to help.

What age can my child start?

We welcome children from 2½ years in Playgroup. Children progress through Lower KG and Upper KG until they are ready for Grade 1.

Will my child be prepared for Grade 1 interviews?

Yes. Our Upper KG programme includes reading, writing, numbers, general knowledge and confidence-building that prepare children for Grade 1 admission at government and private schools.

Which languages are used in class?

English is the main classroom language, with Sinhala and Tamil used and taught so every child feels at home.

Do you celebrate all religions?

Absolutely. Vesak, Deepavali, Christmas, Ramazan and Hajj, Thai Pongal and Avurudu are all part of our year, and children learn to respect every family and faith.

Is transport available?

A school van service is available for Moratuwa and nearby areas. confirm

How will I know how my child is doing?

Daily updates via WhatsApp, termly written progress reports and regular parent–teacher meetings.

How do you keep children safe?

CCTV-monitored classrooms, ID-based pick-up, first-aid trained staff, healthy snacks and strict daily cleaning routines.
