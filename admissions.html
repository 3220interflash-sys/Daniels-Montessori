Questions parents ask

What age can my child start?

From 2½ years in Playgroup, moving through Lower and Upper KG until Grade 1.

Will my child be ready for Grade 1?

Yes. Upper KG builds reading, writing, numbers and the confidence needed for Grade 1 admission interviews.

Which languages are used?

English in class, with Sinhala and Tamil used and taught.

Do you celebrate all religions?

Yes. Vesak, Deepavali, Christmas, Ramazan, Hajj and Avurudu are all part of our year.

What should I bring to register?

Copies of your child's birth certificate and vaccination card, two passport photos and parent NIC copies.

What about fees and transport?

Fees are paid termly. A school van serves Moratuwa and nearby areas. Contact us for details. confirm
