Welcome to Daniels

More than a pre-school. We're a family.

Founded by Roma Jayaratne and co-founded by Shakira de Cruze, Daniels was built on a simple promise: to care for every child the way we care for our own.

  • Authentic AMI Montessori learning across five areas of development
  • Small classes led by trained, caring directresses
  • Strong preparation for Grade 1 admission interviews
  • Daily updates and a true partnership with parents
