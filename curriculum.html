Curriculum

Learning by doing, the AMI Montessori way

Our prepared classrooms follow the authentic AMI Montessori curriculum, blended with Sri Lankan culture, three languages and thoughtful preparation for Grade 1.

Our approach

Five areas of development

Children choose purposeful work, repeat it until mastered, and build concentration, independence and a lasting love of learning.

🌱

Practical Life

Pouring, dressing, laundry and cleaning days. Independence, coordination and care for the environment.

🎨

Sensorial

Colour, shape, size, texture and sound materials that refine the senses and build focus.

🗣️

Language

Phonics, sandpaper letters, storytelling and early reading in English, Sinhala and Tamil.

🔢

Mathematics

Number rods, golden beads and hands-on materials that make numbers concrete.

🌏

Culture

Geography, science, nature, art, music and the festivals of every Sri Lankan community.

Programmes

A programme for every stage

Lower KG

Ages 3½ – 4½
  • Full Montessori work cycle

Upper KG

Ages 4½ – 5½
  • Reading, writing and maths

Beyond the classroom

Preparing for Grade 1 and beyond

Our Upper KG year focuses on the skills Sri Lankan schools look for at Grade 1 admission, without pressure and through play.

  • Reading, writing and number confidence
  • Speaking clearly in English, Sinhala or Tamil
  • General knowledge, colours, shapes and self-care
  • Mock interviews and confidence building
  • Termly written progress reports and parent meetings
