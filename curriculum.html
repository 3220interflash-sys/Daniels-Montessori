Learning

Learning by doing, the Montessori way

Children choose meaningful work, repeat it until they master it, and grow in focus, independence and joy.

Our approach

Five areas of learning

Practical lifePouring, dressing, cleaning and caring for the classroom
SensorialColour, shape, size and texture to sharpen the senses
LanguagePhonics, stories and early reading in English, Sinhala and Tamil
MathematicsHands-on materials that make numbers real
CultureNature, science, music, art and Sri Lanka's festivals
Programmes

Classes and ages

Playgroup2½ – 3½ yearsSettling in, sensory play, songs, toilet-training support
Lower KG3½ – 4½ yearsThe full Montessori work cycle, phonics and early numbers
Upper KG4½ – 5½ yearsReading, writing, maths and Grade 1 interview readiness
DaycareUntil 5.30 pm confirmLunch, rest, quiet play and homework help

School hours: Monday to Friday, 7.30 am – 1.00 pm

Our year

The year at a glance

Each term follows learning themes, with celebrations from every Sri Lankan community.

Term 1 Jan – Apr

All about me · My family · Community helpers · Sri Lankan traditions

  • First Day welcome
  • Career Day
  • Avurudu games

Thai Pongal · Independence Day · Ramazan · Sinhala & Tamil New Year · Easter

Term 2 May – Aug

Plants · Our Earth · Water & weather · Transport

  • Vesak lantern making
  • Laundry & practical life days
  • Annual Sports Meet

Vesak · Hajj · Poson · Esala Perahera

Term 3 Sep – Dec

Animals · Art & creativity · Space · Giving

  • Art & Craft Exhibition
  • Halloween dress-up
  • Concert & UKG Graduation

Children's Day · Teachers' Day · Deepavali · Christmas

Plus a birthday celebration every month. Exact dates are shared with parents each term.
